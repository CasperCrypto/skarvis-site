Privacy policy

This page explains what happens to the data you enter in the SKARVIS pilot-list form. The Polish version is the binding one.

Data controller

The data controller is [TO FILL IN: name or company name, address]. Contact about your data: [TO FILL IN: email address].

What we collect

Your name, organisation or site, email address, site type, and if you provide them: phone, town, your message and whether you are open to a letter of intent. We also store the date of the submission and the page language.

Purpose and legal basis

  • Contacting you about the pilot, based on your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Preparing a possible pilot at your request, before a contract (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

We do not send newsletters, do not sell data, and do not make automated decisions about you or profile you.

Where it is stored

Submissions go to a Supabase database in the European Union (Frankfurt region). Supabase acts as a processor on our behalf. Only the controller can read submissions.

How long

Until you withdraw consent, and no longer than 12 months after our last contact, unless we sign a pilot agreement. In that case the terms of that agreement apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych).

Giving us your data is voluntary, but without it we cannot contact you.

Cookies and third-party resources

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. Fonts (Google Fonts) and the 3D graphics library (jsDelivr) load from third-party servers, which see your IP address when they do.
